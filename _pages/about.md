I am currently an assistant professor at the [Robotics Group](https://www.hw.ac.uk/uk/schools/engineering-physical-sciences/institutes/sensors-signals-systems/robotics-ocean-systems.htm), Heriot-Watt University, affiliated with the [National Robotarium](https://thenationalrobotarium.com) and the [Edinburgh Center for Robotics](https://www.edinburgh-robotics.org/about-us). I am also the Deputy Director of the [CDT-D2AIR](https://www.edinburgh-robotics.org/apply) and a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow. Prior to that, I was a research associate at the [Autonomous Agents Research Group](https://agents.inf.ed.ac.uk), University of Edinburgh, working with Assoc. Prof. Stefano Albrecht. I obtained my Ph.D. at the [Institute of Automotive Engineering](https://www.fzd.tu-darmstadt.de/fzd/index.en.jsp), Technical University of Darmstadt, supervised by Prof. Hermann Winner. After my Ph.D., I joined DJI Automotive as a senior engineer, working with Assoc. Prof. Yuxin Zhang. 

I am leading the RoboSafe lab, where the lab's vision is to design and deploy safe and trustworthy robotics to benefit human beings and society. My research interests are AI safety, safety assurance of autonomous vehicles, scenario-based testing and world models, driver behavior modeling, and explainable AI.

I am recruiting PhD students under the [SPADS CDT](https://spads.eng.ed.ac.uk/) and the [CDT-D2AIR](https://www.edinburgh-robotics.org/apply) and also welcome undergraduate and masters students for internships. Drop me a message if you are interested. We currently recruit a [postdoctoral researcher in robotics and AI](https://enzj.fa.em3.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX/job/3993?utm_source=eurosciencejobs&utm_medium=job_board&utm_campaign=apply_now_job_link).
